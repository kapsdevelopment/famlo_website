Help with Famlo Kids

During testing, these instructions apply to families with access to Famlo Kids and “Children and family” in the parent app.

How do I connect a new phone?

Open Famlo Kids on your child’s phone. If you see “This phone is disconnected”, tap “Show new code”. Open the Famlo parent app on your own device, select “Scan QR code” and scan the code on your child’s screen. Choose the child’s existing profile and tap “Connect”. Your child will see their avatar and calendar again. You can also start scanning from “Children and family”.

Why can a sibling see the activity?

When a child adds “Swimming”, it appears in their “My day” and in the “Family” view. Siblings will see it in their own “My day” if they are selected as participants or the activity is for the whole family.

How do I disconnect a lost phone?

Open “Children and family” in the Famlo parent app. Select “Phones” for the right child, find the phone and confirm that you want to disconnect it. This blocks that phone’s access to Famlo. The child keeps their profile, activities and other approved phones. An offline phone can still show its last saved day view until it reconnects.

How do I permanently delete a child profile?

Open “Children and family” in the Famlo parent app and choose “Delete profile” for the right child. Confirm permanent deletion. The child’s phones are disconnected and their profile and personal data are deleted. Shared activities may stay in the family calendar with the link to their creator removed. If you only removed the child from the family, the profile is kept and you can restore their membership.

Delete account or data

Delete in the app or ask for help here.

If you are signed in to Famlo, you can delete your account directly in the app where account deletion is available. You can also use the contact form below and choose Delete account or data if you need help, cannot access the app, or only want specific data removed.

Please describe whether you want the whole account deleted or only specific information or content. If you want a reply, use the email address connected to the account, or tell us which email the account uses.

Avoid sending sensitive information you do not need to share. If you are unsure how to phrase the request, it is enough to briefly write what you want deleted.

FAQ

What people usually wonder about first

If you do not find what you need here, use the contact form above.

How do I report a bug in Famlo?

Use the contact form on this page and choose Bug report. Briefly describe what happened, what you expected and which device you used.

Can I contact you even if I am not signed in?

Yes. You can use the support form even if you are not signed in or only use the app in guest mode.

What should I include in a bug report?

A short description of the problem is most helpful. If possible, include when it happened, the device and the operating system version.

How do I request deletion of my account or data?

You can delete your account in the app if you are signed in and the function is available. You can also choose Delete account or data in the form and write what you want deleted. If you want to delete only some of your data, tell us which information you want removed.

Can I send ideas for new features?

Yes, please. Famlo is made for real family days, so ideas from families are especially useful when we prioritize what comes next.

What if information in the app looks wrong?

Tell us which card showed the information and when you saw it. This helps us find and fix the problem.
