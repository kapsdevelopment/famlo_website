Why Famlo

Built for real family days

Famlo should not feel like another app demanding attention. It is made to reduce friction, make information easier to read and help the family stay oriented without keeping everything in their heads.

Less friction

The essentials first

Stop jumping between apps just to know what is happening today, what the weather looks like and how everyone gets where they need to go.

Quick to read

Clear even when mornings are busy

Large surfaces, strong contrasts and a warm visual style make information easier to scan when the day is already moving.

Shared flow

More overview, less coordination

When the family shares a clear overview, it becomes easier to follow plans, appointments and small changes through the day.

Friendly help

Support when your head is full

Famlo is made to provide short summaries and useful help when many things are happening at home at the same time.
