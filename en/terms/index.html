Famlo Kids and the role of parents

As a parent or guardian, you set up your child’s profile in the Famlo parent app. You choose the profile and approve each phone that will have access. Only show the pairing code to the adult who will scan it. If a phone is lost or no longer in use, you can disconnect it under “Children and family”.

Your child can add an activity directly to the calendar. The family can see it as soon as it is saved. “My day” shows activities for the selected person. “Family” shows activities shared in the family calendar. Help your child decide what to share there.
