Privacy notice for Famlo and Famlo Kids

How Famlo and Famlo Kids use information about you and your family.

Updated 26 November 2026 · Norsk · Suomi

KAPSDEVELOPMENT AS (Norwegian organisation number 937 284 624) is the data controller for Famlo, Famlo Kids and our support service. You can reach us at contact@kapsdevelopment.com.

For children using Famlo Kids · Who can see your information? · Calendar and AI · Your rights

For children using Famlo Kids

If you add “Swimming”, you’ll see it in “My day”. Other people in your family can see it when they open “Family”. Your brother or sister will see it in their own “My day” if you’re both going. An activity for the whole family appears for everyone.

Adding “Swimming” in Famlo Kids sends nothing to artificial intelligence (AI). If an adult later asks Famlo to sum up the day or tell them what’s on today, the AI may then hear about your swimming.

An adult chooses your profile in Famlo and approves your phone. You don’t need your own email address or password. You can ask an adult for help if an activity looks wrong or if you want to know what your family can see.

For parents and guardians

To connect a phone to the right child, we use a QR code that expires, a sign-in managed by the app and an identifier for that installation of the app. Famlo records which phone you approve and which profile it belongs to. We use the display name, chosen avatar and family membership to show the right day and participants.

We store activities on our server so that changes appear across the family’s devices. An activity can include a title, date, time, participants, who added it and an optional location or note. Kids stores sign-in details, the most recently loaded day views and any drafts on the device. If you disconnect a phone while it is offline, it can still show information it has already loaded until the app reconnects and learns that access has been revoked.

The weather forecast in Kids uses the location and time zone the family has chosen. Parents manage these settings in Famlo. Famlo Kids is being tested. Before general release, we will update this notice with information about the legal basis for processing children’s data and any transfers outside the European Economic Area (EEA).

What information does the Famlo parent app use?

  • Sign-in and profile. We use your email address, an identifier from the sign-in service, a display name if provided, your household name and time zone to give you access to the right account and family.
  • Weather, public transport and driving routes. Guests can check the weather and public transport departures. In guest mode, your choices are usually stored on your device. Saved places and routes can include place names, coordinates, stops and addresses.
  • Calendar and family. We use the profiles, activities and participants that family members enter to display and update the calendar. If someone adds you as a participant, that information comes from the person who entered the activity.
  • Avatars, purchases and subscriptions. If you ask us to create an avatar from a drawing or photo, the image may be processed to create the avatar. For purchases in the parent app, we use information from the app store to confirm the purchase and provide access. Children can choose from the available avatars in Kids.

Who can see your information?

Your family can see what is shared in the family calendar. We use Supabase for sign-in, the database and storage. Weather, transport, map and address search providers receive the information needed for the particular lookup. The app store handles payments for purchases.

Avatar images. If you ask us to generate an avatar, the provider that creates it may receive the image you submit.

Support. Our contact form uses Formcarry to forward your name, email address, selected app, subject and message. We use these details to reply and follow up. You can also email us directly. Please leave QR codes and the contents of your child’s calendar out of your message.

When does Famlo share calendar information with AI?

Famlo does not generate a daily summary automatically. Opening or refreshing the home screen in the parent app can show the last saved summary without sending calendar information to AI.

  • When you request a daily summary. If you tap “Generate” in Flagre, Famlo sends today’s ongoing and upcoming activities to the AI provider to create the summary. These can include activities a child added in Famlo Kids.
  • When you chat with Flagre. The AI provider receives your message and the most recent messages in the conversation. When Famlo recognises a question about what’s on today, it also sends today’s ongoing and upcoming activities. A question about whether you need an umbrella can be answered using the weather forecast without looking up the calendar. An activity mentioned earlier may still appear in the messages sent with your question.

When the calendar is used, the AI provider may receive an activity’s title, date, time and any notes. It may also receive the family and calendar names and technical IDs for the family, calendar, activity and the person who created or edited it.

Each time you send a chat message, Famlo’s own server also receives a copy of the information from the home screen. The server uses the wording of your latest message to decide what information to look up for the AI response.

Storage, disconnecting devices and deletion

We use technical information about how the service runs to fix errors, protect the service and prevent misuse. How long we keep information depends on what it is used for. We use calendar data for as long as it is meant to be available to the user or family. After deletion, data may remain in backups until those backups are replaced in the normal course of operations. We may need to keep some information for longer for security or legal reasons.

  • Disconnect a phone. We block that installation of the app from accessing the server. The child’s profile, activities and other approved phones are kept.
  • Remove a child from the family. The child’s phones lose access to the family. The profile is kept so family membership can be restored. An adult may need to select participants again.
  • Permanently delete a child profile. We delete the profile and the child’s personal data and disconnect all their phones. Shared activities may stay in the family calendar. We remove the link to the child who created the activity. A name someone typed into its title or notes may still appear in that text.

You can delete your own account in Famlo or ask for help deleting specific information. If you are responsible for child profiles, another adult may need to take over first. How to request deletion.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data. Depending on how we process the data, you may also ask us to restrict its use, object to its use or request a copy to transfer elsewhere. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it. A parent or guardian can contact us on a child’s behalf. We may ask for information to confirm who is entitled to access or delete the data.

Email contact@kapsdevelopment.com. You can also complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet). When this notice changes, we update this page and the date at the top.
